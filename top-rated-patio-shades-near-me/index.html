Compare the details, not just the label

Before choosing a provider, ask questions that make the proposed work easier to understand. Keep the answers together so you can compare what each provider has actually described.

Key facts for your comparison:
- Coverage: clarify whether the discussion is about an awning, a patio enclosure, or both, and which parts of the patio are in scope.
- Timing: there isn’t one timeline that applies to every project. Ask what information is needed first and what factors may change the expected schedule.
- Cost: the dimensions, product choice, coverage, and project requirements can affect cost. Ask for the included items and any separate work to be explained; don’t assume two broad estimates cover the same things.
- Coordination: find out how the provider will work with your builder, architect, or designer if they’re part of the project.
- Communication: notice whether your questions receive clear answers and whether the next steps are explained in terms you understand.

A quote describes proposed costs and included work. Scope refers to the products and work being considered. An awning is a covering that extends over an area, while a patio enclosure is intended to enclose a patio space. Ask providers to define any product terms they use differently.
